Frequently Asked Questions

How is Raven Health different? ▾
Raven was founded by a BCBA who understands firsthand how much time therapists lose to manual, repetitive tasks. That’s why we built an all-in-one platform that connects data collection, scheduling, and billing, plus powerful AI features that cut down on things like session notes, so you can focus more on client care, not paperwork.
How much does Raven Health cost? ▾
With our all-in-one Managed Billing Bundle, there’s no out-of-pocket cost to use Raven Health. The software is 100% free, and pricing is a small, flat percentage of monthly claims collected, so you only pay when you get paid. Learn more on our pricing page .
Does Raven Health have an offline mode? ▾
Yes, Raven’s app has both online and offline mode capabilities.
Who is Raven Health best suitable for? ▾
Raven Health is ideal for ABA startups and small to midsize clinics, including practice owners, BCBAs, RBTs, administrative teams, and school or education based programs, who need a mobile first platform that keeps clinical and operational workflows running smoothly.
Does your software offer mobile-friendly ABA data collection? ▾
Yes. Raven Health offers fully mobile-friendly ABA data collection on both iOS and Android. Clinicians can record accurate data from any device, whether they’re working in homes, schools, or the clinic. Everything syncs securely so BCBAs can review progress and generate reports without extra steps.
Does Raven Health include a flexible ABA scheduling system with mobile access? ▾
Yes. Raven Health includes a flexible scheduling system that works smoothly on mobile devices. Staff can view schedules, confirm sessions, and manage appointments on the go, making it easier for teams to stay organized across locations and shift changes.
